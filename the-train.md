## Derby Lightweight overview

The group preserves Derby Lightweight vehicles 79018 and 79612. Both entered British Railways service in January 1955, originally with different partners. After passenger service, they were retained for conversion into an ultrasonic test train, before the DLPG's rescue campaign brought them into preservation in 1994.

This chronology presents the history recorded by the Derby Lightweight Preservation Group (DLPG), including references to *Pioneers*, the group's magazine. A month or reporting period does not establish an exact day.

## Vehicle 79018

The power car, M79018, entered service on **22 January 1955**, paired with **M79610**, at Carlisle Upperby. Its passenger-service history includes allocations to Workington and a return to Carlisle Upperby in 1965.

## Vehicle 79612

The trailer car, M79612, entered service on **29 January 1955**, paired with **M79020**, at Carlisle Upperby. It followed a similar allocation history to 79018, with a short loan to Monument Lane in early 1957. The two vehicles preserved by the group were therefore not originally delivered as a pair.

## Service history

Both vehicles moved to Workington in the reporting period ending 16 July 1955 and returned to Carlisle Upperby in the period ending 28 February 1965. The history recorded by the DLPG dates 79018's passenger withdrawal in the period ending 8 June 1968 and 79612's on 16 June 1968.

On 1 November 1970, both transferred to departmental stock for conversion into an ultrasonic test train. Their subsequent allocation history includes Old Oak Common and Reading.

## Restoration and preservation history

The DLPG formed in late 1990 to rescue and restore the surviving twin. A fundraising campaign led to acquisition in 1994, followed by asbestos removal at Doncaster, recorded as completed in October 1995. The vehicles arrived at the Midland Railway Centre (MRC) at Butterley on 17–18 March 1997.

On 4 September 2004, the power car moved under its own power on one engine at the group's AGM. The vehicles moved from the Midland Railway Centre to the Ecclesbourne Valley Railway (EVR) on 24 November 2014, and the trailer car, 79612, was launched into traffic on 12 April 2025.
