Derby Lightweight vehicles 79018 and 79612 entered passenger service with different partners in 1955. Their later lives took them into departmental service as an ultrasonic test train, then into preservation through the DLPG's rescue campaign.

This chronology presents the history recorded by the Derby Lightweight Preservation Group (DLPG), including references to *Pioneers*, the group's magazine. A month or reporting period does not establish an exact day.

## Passenger service

The history recorded by the DLPG dates the first units of this batch appearing in October 1954. The group's two vehicles entered service the following January. Workington's depot-code changes below represent changes of code, rather than additional transfers.

### M79018

| Date or period | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 22 January 1955 | Entered service paired with M79610, allocated to Carlisle Upperby (12A). |
| Period 4, week ending 16 July 1955 | Transferred to Workington (12D). |
| October 1955 | Workington's depot code changed to 12C. |
| April 1958 | Workington's depot code changed again, to 11B. |
| Period 3, week ending 28 February 1965 | Transferred to Carlisle Upperby (12B). |
| Period 4, week ending 8 June 1968 | Withdrawn from passenger service. |

### M79612

| Date or period | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 29 January 1955 | Entered service paired with M79020, allocated to Carlisle Upperby (12A). |
| Period 4, week ending 16 July 1955 | Transferred to Workington (12D). |
| October 1955 | Workington's depot code changed to 12C. |
| January 1957 | On loan to Monument Lane (3E). |
| February 1957 | Returned from loan to Workington (12C). |
| April 1958 | Workington's depot code changed to 11B. |
| Period 3, week ending 28 February 1965 | Transferred to Carlisle Upperby (12B). |
| 16 June 1968 | Withdrawn from passenger service. |

Both tables present the British Railways service history recorded by the DLPG.

The DLPG records the same subsequent allocation history for both vehicles following their transfer to departmental stock.

| Date or period | Event |
| --- | --- |

| December 1975 | Transferred to Old Oak Common (OOC). |
| 3 March 1980 | Transferred to Reading (RG). |

These allocations form part of the British Railways service history recorded by the DLPG. The rescue chronology below records release for disposal in 1992 and acquisition in 1994 as separate milestones.

## Restoration and preservation

The history recorded by the DLPG traces restoration and moves to the Midland Railway Centre (MRC) at Butterley and the Ecclesbourne Valley Railway (EVR).

| Date or period | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| August 1995 | A pair of AEC engines was purchased. | DLPG history |
| October 1995 | Asbestos removal was completed and certificates were sent. | DLPG history |
| 17–18 March 1997 | The unit arrived at the Midland Railway Centre (MRC) at Butterley from Doncaster. | *Pioneers*, issue 37 |
| September 1997 | A DLPG committee was established to run the group. | *Pioneers*, issue 41 |
| November 2003 | A Heritage Lottery Fund (HLF) grant was obtained. | DLPG history |
| 4 September 2004 | At the AGM, the power car moved under its own power on one engine, recorded as its first powered movement in 15 years. | DLPG history |
| 15–17 October 2004 | The DLPG history records the “SVR Railcar 50” gala. | DLPG history |
| Date not recorded | Trailer-car restoration began. | DLPG history |
| 24 November 2014 | The unit moved from the Midland Railway Centre at Butterley to the Ecclesbourne Valley Railway (EVR). | DLPG history |
| 20 July 2016 | The stores vehicle followed. | DLPG history |
| Date not recorded | Progress accelerated, but part of the power car's interior needed removal to attend to roof leaks. | DLPG history |
| 12 April 2025 | Trailer car M79612 was launched into traffic. | DLPG history |

The DLPG's historical account records continuing power-car restoration following the trailer's launch. For the separately maintained current summary, [visit Restoration]({{ '/restoration/' | relative_url }}).
